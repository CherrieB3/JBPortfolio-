№ 01 — About

About

I'm a product designer and digital artist from McKinney, Texas, currently studying at UT Dallas. I've always found myself drawn to the space where art, usability, and storytelling meet — and that's exactly where I try to design: interfaces that feel intuitive and functional, but still full of personality.

My background is rooted in digital art, and it shows in how I approach a project — I care as much about how something feels to use as whether it works. In practice, that means starting from how people actually interact with what's in front of them, then designing toward something that's both practical and genuinely enjoyable.

Outside of formal projects, I'm pretty much always drawing — sketchbooks, doodles, whatever's on my mind that day. It's part of why illustration and storytelling keep finding their way into my UX work, and probably why there's a rabbit hiding somewhere on every page of this site.
