About

Hey! I'm Jasmine! A UX designer and digital artist from McKinney, Texas, studying at UT Dallas. I been interested in design all my life, and have been working on it professionally for a few years now.

Outside of formal projects, I'm pretty much always drawing! I do commission work, and personal experimental work too! That's part of why illustration keeps finding its way into my UX work, and why I've embraced this style as part of my design identity. It allows me to blend my love for illustration with UX design!
