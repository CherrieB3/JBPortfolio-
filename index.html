About

I design for the people products tend to forget.

I'm Jasmine, a product designer finishing my degree in Arts, Technology, and Emerging Communication at UT Dallas. My projects start with someone who's struggling: a blind visitor hunting for elevator buttons, a commuter circling a full lot before class, a ranger with five tools that don't talk to each other. I research what's really in their way, then design the system around them, not just the screen.

This summer at PassionWorks, I worked alongside artists with developmental disabilities to launch a new owl plush product line, and designed the production templates and workflows behind it. It's the kind of work I want to keep doing: listening first, then making things easier for everyone involved.

I'm also an illustrator, taking commissions since 2021. Drawing shapes how I design: I sketch before I wireframe, I care about type and color, and I want an interface to feel good, not just work.
